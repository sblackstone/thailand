Chiang Mai — the north

WED 4 → SAT 7 NOV

Wake up in the mountains. Lanna temples, an ethical elephant day, jungle waterfalls and cool air — the exhale after Bangkok's heat. Three nights at U Chiang Mai, inside the moat.

The room is booked from Tue 3 Nov, the night you're on the train. That night is paid for deliberately: the room is already yours when you step off the sleeper at 07:15 on the 4th, so you walk in and check in on time whatever the train does. The rate is non-refundable, so the dates can't be shifted. Breakfast isn't included — about THB 399 each for the buffet.
Heads-up on lanterns: the famous Yi Peng / Loy Krathong festival lands about Nov 24–25 this year — roughly ten days after you fly home. You'll narrowly miss the sky lanterns, so plan Chiang Mai around its year-round temples and jungle rather than the festival.

Krabi — the Andaman

SAT 7 → TUE 10 NOV

Swap mountains for limestone and sea. Longtail boats, castaway beaches, and karst cliffs rising straight out of turquoise water. Three nights at Centara Ao Nang, on the beach.

You land well before check-in. FD3072 puts you in Krabi at 09:55 and the room isn't ready until 15:00, so plan to leave the bags at reception and start the day on the beach. Two things worth asking the hotel for when you arrive: a room away from the drainage canal that runs between the wings — it's a known smell complaint — and confirmation of the pool-access room you booked. Rate is non-refundable; breakfast is extra, around THB 325–650 each.

Khao Sok — on the lake

TUE 10 → FRI 13 NOV

Three nights floating on Cheow Lan Lake at 360° Issara, ringed by limestone towers in one of the oldest rainforests on earth. The centrepiece of the trip.

Transfers are included. The package covers a private car from Krabi (~2 h) to the 500Rai Pier Office at Chiew Larn, then a 1.5 h longtail. Scheduled boats leave the pier at 12:00 or 15:30 only. Pack small — you climb down stairs to the boat, and big cases can stay at the pier office. Bring THB 340 each in cash for the national park fee.
